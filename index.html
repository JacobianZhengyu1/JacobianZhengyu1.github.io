I am a Ph.D. student at Texas A&M University. My research focuses on robot learning, embodied AI, and vision-language-action (VLA) models for contact-rich and generalizable robotic manipulation.

Previously, I received my M.S. in Mechanical Engineering from the University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign in 2025, where I worked on humanoid robot teleoperation, multi-robot collaborative manufacturing, and robotic perception.

Research Interests: Vision-Language-Action Models, Tactile Robot Learning, Mobile Manipulation, Reinforcement Learning, and Embodied AI.

ForeTac-VLA: A Forecasting-Based Tactile-Vision-Language-Action Model for Contact-Rich Robotic Manipulation
Zhengyu Tao, Xin Li, Xin Wang
arXiv preprint, 2026
webpage | paper

ForeTac-VLA introduces forecasting-based tactile conditioning for contact-rich manipulation, combining temporal tactile encoding, bidirectional cross-attention, and multi-step tactile prediction to improve action generation.
